Why you'll only see fresh roles here

Every role on this site was posted in the last 14 days and was still open the last time we checked.

Why apply early?

Recruiters often start reading applications as soon as a role opens. Applying early puts your application in front of them while they're still looking, which makes it more likely they'll get back to you.

Old postings waste your time

A posting from last month is often already decided. Instead of letting you write a cover letter for a role that's gone, we take it down when the company closes it or when it turns 14 days old.

Quick vs. long applications

Some companies use systems like Greenhouse or Lever: one page, upload your resume, done in minutes. Others use Workday or Taleo, where you make an account and retype your resume. We label each role so you can plan your time.

How it works

  1. Every 15 minutes, we check the career sites of 5,000+ companies directly.
  2. We keep US electrical engineering internships, co-ops and new grad roles.
  3. We re-check every role every 15 minutes and remove it once it closes or turns 14 days old.
  4. If you've set up alerts, new matches go straight to your inbox.
